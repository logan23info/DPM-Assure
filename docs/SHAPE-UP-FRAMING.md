# Shape Up Framing

## Problem
Privacy assurance work is fragmented across laws, frameworks, spreadsheets, evidence stores, workpapers, findings, and review processes. DPM-Assure creates one traceable assurance chain without collapsing legal truth, methodology, and AI assistance into one source.

## Appetite
Build in small, independently reviewable slices. Foundation work is completed before production application workflows.

## Foundation bet
Establish the governed vocabulary, source registry, framework/version model, control methodology, domain model, deterministic rules, PostgreSQL schema, security invariants, and testing strategy.

## Subsequent bets
1. Identity and organization tenancy.
2. Framework and control library.
3. Engagement planning and risk.
4. Workpapers, procedures, sampling, and PBC.
5. Evidence integrity and Evidence Gate.
6. Testing, findings, remediation, and retest.
7. Review, sign-off, reporting, and freeze.
8. Privacy operations and continuous monitoring.
9. AI assistance with full traceability.

## No-go shortcuts
Do not start with dashboard polish, fake authentication, client-controlled actor identity, ungoverned framework content, direct mutable conclusions, or AI-generated compliance claims.
